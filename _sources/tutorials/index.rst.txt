Tutorials
=========

A handful of longer, worked walkthroughs that cross domain boundaries --
each one uses two or more of mathkit's 14 subpackages together on a
single running example.

.. grid:: 1 2 2 2
   :gutter: 2

   .. grid-item-card:: Newton's Method, Twice
      :link: /tutorials/newtons_method_across_domains
      :link-type: doc

      Root-finding in :mod:`mathkit.numerical_analysis` and Newton/BFGS
      optimization in :mod:`mathkit.optimization` are the same idea,
      applied to a gradient instead of a function.

   .. grid-item-card:: Randomness, Three Ways
      :link: /tutorials/randomness_three_ways
      :link-type: doc

      Monte Carlo integration and Markov chains in
      :mod:`mathkit.probability`, cross-checked against the bootstrap in
      :mod:`mathkit.statistics`.

   .. grid-item-card:: Eigenvalues Everywhere
      :link: /tutorials/eigenvalues_everywhere
      :link-type: doc

      The same eigenvalue problem shows up in :mod:`mathkit.linalg`'s
      decompositions, :mod:`mathkit.graph_theory`'s spectral clustering,
      and :mod:`mathkit.ode_dynamics`'s stability analysis.

   .. grid-item-card:: From Smooth Chaos to Fractal Geometry
      :link: /tutorials/chaos_and_fractals
      :link-type: doc

      The logistic map's period-doubling cascade in
      :mod:`mathkit.ode_dynamics` and the Lyapunov exponents/fractal
      dimension that quantify it in :mod:`mathkit.fractals_chaos`.

   .. grid-item-card:: Counting, Primes, and Probability
      :link: /tutorials/counting_primes_and_probability
      :link-type: doc

      :mod:`mathkit.combinatorics`'s counting formulas,
      :mod:`mathkit.number_theory`'s primes, and the binomial
      distribution in :mod:`mathkit.probability` they all secretly share.
