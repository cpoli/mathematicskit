His proof used random codes: draw 2^{nR} codewords at random and decode to the nearest one. Here the rate is fixed at R = 1/4 and the block length grows: below capacity the error falls toward zero, above capacity it rises toward one.">

Shannon&#x27;s rate-distortion function R(D) is the fewest bits per symbol that reproduce a source within average distortion D: for a fair coin with Hamming distortion R(D) = 1 - h(D), and for a Gaussian with squared error R(D) = \tfrac12\log_2(\sigma^2/D), 6.02 dB of quality per bit.">
