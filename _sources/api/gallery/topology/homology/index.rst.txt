The invariant factors classify the abelian group \mathbb{Z}^m / A\mathbb{Z}^n: a factor 1 kills a generator, d &gt; 1 leaves a cyclic group \mathbb{Z}/d, and missing factors leave free \mathbb{Z} summands. The same reduction applied to a boundary matrix gives a space&#x27;s torsion.">
