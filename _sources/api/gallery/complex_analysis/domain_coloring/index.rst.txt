Domain coloring
---------------

Phase portraits: coloring each point z by the phase and modulus of f(z).

A zero is where all colors meet counter-clockwise, a pole where they meet clockwise, and the order is the number of times each color appears around the point. The technique was named by Frank Farris in the 1990s and developed systematically by Elias Wegert.">
