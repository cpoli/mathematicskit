Hierholzer (1873) proved the converse for a connected graph, and his proof is an algorithm: walk along unused edges until you are stuck, which can only happen back where you started; then, from any vertex of that closed walk that still has unused edges, walk a second closed tour and splice it in. Repeat until no edge is left.">
