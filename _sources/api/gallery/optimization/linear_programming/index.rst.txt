Karmarkar (1984) cut through its interior instead, with an algorithm that was both polynomial-time and fast in practice. Its descendants, the primal-dual path-following methods used by every modern LP solver, track the central path: the points where every product x_i s_i of a primal variable and its dual slack equals the same \mu. As \mu \to 0 the path ends at the optimal vertex.">
