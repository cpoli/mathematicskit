Constrained optimization

Lagrange multipliers and the KKT conditions, the Frank-Wolfe method, and

.. raw:: html

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Minimizes x^2+y^2 subject to x+y=1. At the constrained optimum (0.5, 0.5) the objective&#x27;s gradient is parallel to the constraint&#x27;s gradient, \nabla f = -\lambda \nabla h with \lambda=-1, which is Lagrange&#x27;s condition. Rewriting the constraint as the inequality x+y \geq 1 gives a Karush-Kuhn-Tucker problem whose multiplier must also be non-negative.">
