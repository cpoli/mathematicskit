Iterative solvers
-----------------

Conjugate gradient (SPD systems) and GMRES (general systems): Krylov
subspace methods that never form a dense factorization.

Also the classical stationary iterations: Jacobi, Gauss-Seidel, and
successive over-relaxation (SOR).
