Polynomials
-----------

Evaluating polynomials efficiently with Horner's scheme, and how
sensitive their roots can be to tiny changes in the coefficients.

Its intermediate values are the coefficients of the quotient p(x) / (x - x_0), which gives the derivative for Newton&#x27;s method and lets a found root be divided out (&quot;deflation&quot;). This script finds all roots of a quartic that way and compares operation counts.">
