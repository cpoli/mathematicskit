Numerical integration
---------------------

The explicit integrators behind every trajectory in this gallery: Euler's
method, Runge-Kutta, Stormer-Verlet, Dormand-Prince adaptive stepping,
Yoshida's symplectic composition, and the Adams-Bashforth multistep
methods and their stability regions.

Evaluating the slope at the end of the step instead gives the implicit (backward) variant y_{n+1} = y_n + h f(t_{n+1}, y_{n+1}). Both have a local error of O(h^2), which adds up to a global error of O(h): halving the step halves the error.">
