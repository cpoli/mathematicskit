    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Galois turned the question &quot;is this equation solvable?&quot; into a question about a group of permutations of its roots. The roots of x^3 - 2 are \sqrt[3]{2}, \sqrt[3]{2}\,\omega and \sqrt[3]{2}\,\omega^2 (with \omega = e^{2\pi i/3}). Complex conjugation swaps the two non-real roots, multiplication by \omega cycles all three, and closing these two permutations under composition gives Galois&#x27;s group -- the full symmetric group S_3, which is non-abelian.">
