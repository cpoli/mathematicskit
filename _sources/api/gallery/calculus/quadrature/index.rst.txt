Quadrature
----------

Riemann sums, Simpson's rule, Gauss-Legendre, Euler-Maclaurin, Romberg,
Clenshaw-Curtis, tanh-sinh, and QUADPACK-style adaptive quadrature.

This example shows the nodes and weights from legendre_nodes_and_weights, checks the degree of exactness of GaussianQuadrature, and compares its convergence with the equally spaced trapezoidal rule.">
